House Rules

The oldest mark goes dim when it is next in line to vanish.

  • FIFO Each player keeps only three live marks. On the fourth placement, their oldest mark disappears at the same moment.
  • Random opener Every new round starts with a coin flip. In AI modes, you are always X and the AI is always O.
